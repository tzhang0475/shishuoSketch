```text
Story → Person → Relation → Related Story → Era
```

| Area | Target | Current status |
|---|---:|---|
| Core people | 6 | carried forward from the existing six-person pilot |

| Eras / motifs | at least 1 | `era-001` is a candidate based on `雅量` |

from names, titles, URLs, or generated prose.
