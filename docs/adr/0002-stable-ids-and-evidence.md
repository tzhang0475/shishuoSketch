# ADR 0002: Stable IDs and evidence-linked data

## Decision

Entity IDs are assigned identifiers, not names or generated labels. Existing
stable IDs are preserved. Historical and interpretive records carry explicit
assertion and review status, and important claims point to separate Evidence
records.

## Consequences

Aliases can preserve orthographic and title variants without changing source
text. Unresolved mentions remain unresolved. Regenerating candidates or
derived pages does not silently create new identities or erase provenance.
